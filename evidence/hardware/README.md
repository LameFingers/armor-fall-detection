# Hardware Evidence

Photographs documenting the physical build of the ARMOR prototype.

---

## Receiver board

**breadboard1-receiver-build** — Receiver (LILYGO T3 V1.6.1) assembled on breadboard during initial build and functional test — RGB LED, KY-006 buzzer, acknowledge button, and LoRa antenna.

![Receiver breadboard build](breadboard1-receiver-build.jpg)

---

**receiver-closeup** — Close-up of the finished receiver board showing component placement and wiring.

![Receiver close-up](receiver-closeup.jpg)

## Sender board

**breadboard2-sender-build** — Sender (LILYGO T3 V1.6.1 + DFRobot Gravity BMI160) assembled on breadboard — IMU, RGB LED, KY-006 buzzer, cancel button, and LiPo battery connector.

![Sender breadboard build](breadboard2-sender-build.jpg)

---

**breadboard3-sender-build** — Sender breadboard build from a second angle — showing BMI160 I²C wiring and power connections.

![Sender breadboard build angle 2](breadboard3-sender-build.jpg)

---

**sender-closeup** — Close-up of the finished sender (wearable) board showing the full assembly.
